§ 05
FAQ

Questions
I get a lot.

Ask anything ↓
Q/01How quickly can you start?+
Usually within 1–2 weeks of agreeing scope. I keep one freelance slot open at any time alongside my day job, so I never overcommit. If I can't take it on right away, I'll tell you on the consult call — not after we've signed.
Q/02Do you work nights and weekends?+
Yes — that's when freelance happens. I have a full-time job at Avrioc, and I treat freelance as a serious second commitment, not a hobby. You'll get steady weekly progress and regular updates.
Q/03What if I don't have data yet?+
Common starting point. We use the consult to figure out what data you'd need, where it might live, and whether collecting it is even worth it. Sometimes the right answer is "don't build the AI yet." I'll tell you that.
Q/04Do you sign NDAs?+
Of course. Send yours over before the consult and I'll review it. Standard mutual NDAs are signed within a day; anything more involved goes through a lawyer.
Q/05Who owns the code and the model?+
You do. By default, on full payment, all source code, model weights, and documentation transfer to you with no strings. The only thing I keep is the right to mention the project (anonymized if you prefer) in my portfolio.
Q/06Can you work with my existing team?+
Yes — I integrate with internal devs, data teams, or another agency regularly. Slack, Jira, GitHub, GitLab, whatever you use. I document so the next person picking it up isn't lost.
Q/07What's not a good fit?+
Anything that needs a 10-person team, anything where "AI" is being added because investors want to hear it, or anything illegal / surveillance-adjacent. I'll happily refer you elsewhere if I think that's the right move.
